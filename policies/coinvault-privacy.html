CoinVault — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026 App: CoinVault (in.sgkslabs.coinvault) Contact: sgkslabs@gmail.com

1. Introduction

SGKS Labs ("we", "us", "our") built CoinVault as a local-first application for cataloguing coin collections. This Privacy Policy explains what information CoinVault collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using CoinVault, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Summary

  • Your coin collection and photos are stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • CoinVault does not require an account and does not run its own analytics or tracking.
  • Free-tier use shows ads served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which does process certain device data.
  • Google Drive backup/sync is a Premium, opt-in feature — nothing is uploaded until you connect it yourself.
  • We never sell or share your collection data with anyone.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Create and Control

Coin records, photos (front/back/edge/detail and profile photo), and collector-profile details (name, about, notes) that you enter into CoinVault. This data is stored locally on your device in CoinVault's private app storage and, only if you enable it, in your own Google Drive account. SGKS Labs never receives a copy of this data on our own servers — we don't operate any backend server for CoinVault.

3.2 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

CoinVault integrates three Google services, each of which independently processes limited data:

  • Google Play Billing — active when you purchase Premium. Handles all payment details itself; CoinVault only receives a purchase-status confirmation (Premium / Free).
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — Free tier only, removed entirely on Premium. Processes device and advertising identifiers and general device information, to request and display ads.
  • Google Sign-In / Drive API — Premium, opt-in only, active after you tap Connect. Requests the minimal "drive.file" scope: access only to files CoinVault itself creates in your Drive, never your existing files.

Google's own privacy practices for these services are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

3.3 Information We Do Not Collect

CoinVault does not collect or transmit analytics, crash reports, usage tracking, or telemetry of any kind. We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifier unless you choose to enter it into your own collector profile, which stays on your device or your own Drive only.

4. Permissions CoinVault Requests

  • Camera — to photograph your coins directly within the app, used only when you tap the camera button.
  • Internet / Network state — used only for Google Play Billing purchases, loading ads for Free-tier users, and optional Google Drive backup/sync. Every core cataloguing feature works fully offline and never uses these permissions.

CoinVault does not request contacts, location, microphone, SMS, call-log, or broad storage/media permissions. Photo selection uses Android's built-in Photo Picker, which grants CoinVault access only to the specific photo you choose.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your collection lives in a local database and a private app-storage folder that only CoinVault can access, protected by Android's app sandboxing. If you enable Google Drive backup, your data is additionally stored in your own Google Drive account under your Google account's own security. We do not operate any server that stores your collection data.

6. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • Export your entire collection to a local backup file at any time (Settings → Import/Export), independent of us.
  • Delete individual coins or your entire collection at any time within the app.
  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time — this stops all Drive access immediately and never deletes anything stored locally.
  • Uninstalling CoinVault removes its local data from your device, subject to your own Android backup/restore settings.

7. Children's Privacy

CoinVault is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through CoinVault, contact us and we will address it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above. Continued use of CoinVault after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
