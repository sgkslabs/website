Salary Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 Developer: SGKS Labs Contact: sgkslabs@gmail.com Package: in.sgkslabs.salarytracker

1. Overview

Salary Tracker ("the app") is a personal salary and payslip tracking app built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles, and the one optional feature that involves a third party: Google Drive backup.

2. The Short Version

  • We collect nothing. There is no backend server operated by the developer, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
  • Your salary data stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.
  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.

3. What Data the App Stores, and Where

All the information you enter — your salary profile (currency, financial-year start month, pay day), the income and deduction heads you define, and every month's entered amounts — is stored locally in a private database on your device. This data stays there by default and is never visible to the developer.

4. Permissions the App Requests, and Why

PermissionWhy it's used
Internet Used only if you choose to connect Google Drive backup (see Section 5). Every other feature works fully offline.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, SMS, call logs, or broad storage/file access. App Lock's optional fingerprint/face unlock uses Android's built-in biometric prompt directly — the app never sees or stores your biometric data itself.

5. Optional: Google Drive Backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive from Settings, the app will:

  • Ask you to grant access with your Google account, using Google's own account and consent screens (the app never sees or stores your Google password).
  • Request access only to a hidden, app-specific storage area in your Drive (the drive.appdata scope) — it can never see, list, or access any other file in your Drive, and the backup file it creates is not visible in your own Drive interface at all.
  • Upload a backup of your salary profile, heads, and monthly entries only when you tap "Back Up Now" — there is no automatic or scheduled Drive upload.

This feature is entirely optional. If you never connect Google Drive, no data ever leaves your device. You can disconnect Google Drive backup at any time from Settings, which revokes the app's access immediately.

6. Local Backup and Export

The app also lets you export a full backup to a file of your choosing at any time (Settings → Export Backup), and optionally keep the last 3 backups automatically on your device. Both stay entirely on your device unless you choose to move or share the exported file yourself — the app does not transmit it anywhere on its own.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data lives on your device, you control it directly:

  • Deleting a month's entry, a salary head, or your salary profile in the app removes it immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data.
  • Disconnecting Google Drive backup stops future backups; a backup already in your Drive's app-data storage is removed when you disconnect, or can be deleted by revoking the app's access from your Google Account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all — everything stays under the user's own control.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page. We encourage checking it periodically.
